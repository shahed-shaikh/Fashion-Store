ATELIERÉ is a fashion store website built with HTML5, Tailwind CSS, and custom CSS. Its visual identity pairs warm neutrals, brown, and terracotta with elegant typography.

The hero introduces the brand through “Wear your story.”

New Arrivals presents four pieces: The Sculpted Coat, Everyday Muse, The Modern Layer, and Soft Structure, with prices. Three collections—The Essentials, The Statement, and The Individual—offer distinct style directions.

An editorial campaign shares the label’s perspective on considered fashion. The brand story explains its philosophy, followed by three values: Considered Forms, Meaningful Details, and Personal Style.

A newsletter section invites visitors to stay connected. The contact area encourages visitors to explore new arrivals or send an enquiry. The footer includes brand information, navigation, and social links.

Built with responsive Tailwind layouts, the site features animated hero elements, floating details, rotating accents, image zoom, hover transitions, and reduced-motion support. ATELIERÉ offers a polished storefront for modern wardrobes.
